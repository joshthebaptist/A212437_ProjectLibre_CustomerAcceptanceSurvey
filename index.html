10
Network Diagram & Dependencies
Precedence Diagramming Method (PDM) — Activity-on-Node (AON)
Network Diagram Method
  • Method: Precedence Diagramming Method (PDM)
  • Approach: Activity-on-Node (AON)
  • Boxes: Represent activities (nodes)
  • Arrows: Represent dependencies between activities
Logical Relationships
  • Finish-to-Start (FS): Most common — successor starts after predecessor finishes
  • Start-to-Start (SS): Successor starts when predecessor starts (parallel work)
  • Finish-to-Finish (FF): Both finish together
  • Start-to-Finish (SF): Rare — successor finishes when predecessor starts
Lead & Lag
  • Lead (negative): Successor starts BEFORE predecessor finishes (e.g., FS-3d)
  • Lag (positive): Successor starts AFTER predecessor finishes (e.g., FS+5d)
  • Example: Task 20SS+2d — Task 21 starts 2 days after Task 20 starts
Activity Types
  • Burst Activity: One activity with multiple successors
  • Merge Activity: One activity with multiple predecessors
