## 2. Project Objective

To conduct a comprehensive customer acceptance survey for XYZ Company's new food/drink product at strategic locations around Kuala Lumpur, gathering consumer feedback on product acceptance and preference compared to similar products in the market, from 1 June 2026 to 28 September 2026.

**SMART Objectives:**
- **Specific:** Conduct customer acceptance survey using questionnaire instrument at selected major shopping malls in Kuala Lumpur
- **Measurable:** Collect minimum 500 valid survey responses across 5 locations
- **Achievable:** Utilize existing research methodology and trained survey team
- **Realistic:** Leverage established relationships with shopping mall management
- **Time-bounded:** 1 June - 28 September 2026

## 5. Technical Requirements

1. **Survey Instrument:** Questionnaire designed using both open-ended and closed-ended questions
2. **Data Collection:** Minimum 100 responses per location (5 locations x 100 = 500 minimum)
3. **Survey Locations:**
   - Jaya Jusco (1 Utama)
   - AEON Mall Shah Alam
   - Bangsar Shopping Center
   - Pavilion Kuala Lumpur
   - Suria KLCC
4. **Data Analysis:** Statistical analysis using SPSS or Excel
5. **Report Format:** Professional report following academic standards with proper citations
6. **Presentation:** PowerPoint presentation for management briefing

---

## 6. Limits and Exclusions

### Limits:
1. Survey scope limited to Kuala Lumpur and surrounding areas only
2. Maximum budget of RM 15,000 for the entire project
3. Survey duration from 1 June - 28 September 2026
4. Only consumer acceptance feedback will be collected (not product testing)
5. Limited to 5 major shopping mall locations

### Exclusions:
1. Product modification or reformulation is NOT included
2. Production cost analysis is NOT included
3. Distribution channel analysis is NOT included
4. Long-term market trend analysis is NOT included
5. Competitor product testing is NOT included
6. Online survey component is NOT included (face-to-face only)

---

## 7. Acceptance Criteria

1. Minimum 500 valid and completed survey questionnaires collected
2. Survey conducted at all 5 designated shopping mall locations
3. Response rate of at least 70% from approached respondents
4. Data analysis completed with statistical significance
5. Final report approved by project supervisor/management
6. All documentation properly archived and submitted
7. Management presentation delivered and approved
