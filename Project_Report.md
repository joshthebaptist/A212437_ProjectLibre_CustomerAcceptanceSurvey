## 1. INTRODUCTION

### 1.1 Background

XYZ Company has successfully developed a new food/drink product that is currently in the testing phase and will be launched soon. As part of the market preparation strategy, the company needs to understand consumer acceptance and preference for this new product compared to similar products currently available in the market.

### 1.2 Problem Statement

Before launching the new product, XYZ Company requires comprehensive data on customer acceptance levels at strategic locations in Kuala Lumpur. Without proper market research, the company risks launching a product that may not meet consumer expectations or preferences.

### 1.3 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. To conduct a customer acceptance survey for XYZ Company's new product
2. To collect feedback from consumers at 5 strategic shopping mall locations in Kuala Lumpur
3. To analyze consumer preferences and acceptance levels
4. To provide recommendations to XYZ Company's management based on findings

### 1.4 Project Scope

This project involves planning and executing a customer acceptance survey using questionnaires at the following locations:

1. Jaya Jusco (1 Utama)
2. AEON Mall Shah Alam
3. Bangsar Shopping Center
4. Pavilion Kuala Lumpur
5. Suria KLCC

The survey will be conducted over a period of 4 months, from 1 June 2026 to 28 September 2026.
